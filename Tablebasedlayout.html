Featured Recipe: Chocolate Cake 🎂

This is a simple and delicious chocolate cake recipe you can try at home.

See Ingredients
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
See Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix dry ingredients.
  3. Add eggs and milk, then stir well.
  4. Pour into baking pan and bake for 30 minutes.

💡 Cooking Tip

Add a pinch of coffee powder to enhance the chocolate flavor!
